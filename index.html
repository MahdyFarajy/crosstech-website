Sectors

Every building type

Waterproofing protects the structure. Thermal insulation cuts cooling demand, which matters most in a climate as hot as Saudi Arabia's.

Residential

Stops seepage that damages interiors, weakens structures and causes mould. Insulation keeps homes cooler and lowers energy bills.

Commercial

Protects offices, retail and public facilities from leak disruptions, lowers operating costs and supports green building certification.

Industrial

Shields machinery, stock and structure from water damage, and keeps controlled environments and large spaces energy-efficient.

Waterproofing & insulation

Most popular systems

The right system depends on the building, its exposure and the material specification. We recommend one after a site inspection.

Bituminous membranes

APP & SBS modified, torch-applied
  • APP: high UV and heat tolerance, ideal for hot climates
  • SBS: flexible, expands and contracts without cracking
  • Primer for adhesion, fully sealed overlaps

PVC membranes

Polyester-reinforced, hot-air welded
  • Excellent UV and chemical resistance
  • Accommodates structural movement
  • Installs over thermal insulation for reflective roofs
Typical service life 20–30 years under optimal conditions

Polyurethane spray foam

Two-component, sprayed on site
  • Very low thermal conductivity
  • Closed-cell structure, seamless and waterproof
  • Lightweight, prevents condensation and mould

EPDM membranes

Synthetic rubber, adhered or fastened
  • Flexible through extreme temperatures
  • UV and ozone resistant
  • Large sheets mean fewer seams
Service life often 30+ years under optimal conditions

TPO membranes

Thermoplastic polyolefin, hot-air welded
  • Reflective white surface lowers roof temperature
  • Chlorine-free and recyclable
  • Resists oils, greases and weather cracking

Liquid bitumen

Brush, roller or spray applied
  • Seamless coat for irregular, hard-to-reach areas
  • Works as primer or main layer
  • Bonds to concrete and metal

Cementitious coatings

Polymer-modified cement
  • Bonds chemically with concrete
  • Handles positive and negative water pressure
  • Applies to slightly damp surfaces

HDPE membranes

High-density polyethylene, thermally welded
  • Fully impermeable to water and chemicals
  • High puncture and tear resistance
  • Continuous welded layer, no open joints
Service life often 50+ years under ideal conditions
Floor protection

Epoxy & microcement

Floors that look right and keep performing under traffic, chemicals and moisture, in homes, workplaces and industrial sites.

Epoxy flooring

Resistant to abrasion, chemicals and moisture. Easy to clean, with custom colours and markings. Suited to warehouses, showrooms, hospitals, factories and car parks.

Microcement

A thin, strong cement-look finish applied over existing floors without removal. Seamless and modern for homes, hotels, galleries, cafés and open-plan spaces.

Our procedure

Assessment to handover

The same eight steps on every job: foundations, basements, roofs, wet areas, water tanks and external surfaces.

01

Project assessment

Site inspection, substrate and moisture checks, and a recommended system.

02

Surface preparation

Cleaning, crack and void repair, drying and priming for adhesion.

03

Material selection

Approved products chosen for potable water, UV or chemical exposure.

04

Application

To manufacturer specs, with extra care at joints, corners and penetrations.

05

Inspection & QC

Checks at each critical stage, plus adhesion and thickness tests.

06

Curing & protection

Proper curing time, then screed, boards or coatings as specified.

07

Testing

Ponding, moisture or pressure tests, documented and shared with you.

08

Handover

Warranties, data sheets, method statements and a final walkthrough.
